## JazzCash Integration

### What is JazzCash?

JazzCash is a popular Pakistani mobile payment service.

**Benefits:**
- Easy for customers (many Pakistanis use JazzCash)
- Fast setup
- Reliable payments
- Good settlement

### Setting Up JazzCash

**Prerequisites:**
- JazzCash merchant account
- Merchant ID
- API credentials

**Setup steps:**

**Step 1: Get JazzCash Account**
1. Visit jazz.com.pk
2. Apply for merchant account
3. Provide business details
4. Get approval (typically 2-3 days)
5. Receive merchant credentials

**Step 2: Configure in MatjarX**
1. Go to "Settings" → "Payment"
2. Click "JazzCash"
3. Enter merchant ID
4. Enter API key
5. Save configuration

**Step 3: Test Payment**
1. Add test product
2. Test checkout process
3. Complete test payment
4. Verify it processes correctly
5. Remove test product

**Step 4: Go Live**
1. Remove test mode
2. Monitor first transactions
3. Ensure payments processing
4. Confirm settlement

### JazzCash Settlement

**How you get paid:**

**Payments received** → **JazzCash holds funds** → **Settlement to your account**

**Settlement timeline:** Typically 2-3 business days

**Settlement account:**
- Bank account linked to JazzCash
- Funds transferred automatically
- Check JazzCash dashboard for status

## Bank Transfer Payments

### Direct Bank Transfers

**If customers pay by bank transfer:**

1. Enable bank transfer option
2. Provide your account details
3. Customer transfers payment
4. You manually confirm order
5. Send confirmation to customer

**Setup:**
1. Go to "Settings" → "Payment"
2. Click "Bank Transfer"
3. Enter your account details:
   - Account name
   - Account number
   - Bank name
   - Branch code (if applicable)
4. Save

### Managing Bank Transfers

**When payment received:**
1. Check your bank account
2. Confirm order in MatjarX
3. Send order confirmation to customer
4. Process shipment or delivery

1. Edit product

## Managing Orders

### Viewing Orders

**In your dashboard:**

### Order Status

3. When shipping: send tracking info
4. Upon delivery: send delivery confirmation
5. Any issues: contact customer immediately

**Out of stock:**
- Refund customer immediately

**Damaged product:**
- Customer reports damage

## FAQ

**Q: What payment methods can I accept?**  
A: JazzCash, bank transfers, and others (contact for more options).

**Q: How much does payment processing cost?**  
A: Depends on gateway (typically 1-3% per transaction). Confirm with your provider.

**Q: How long until money is in my account?**  
A: Typically 2-3 business days depending on payment method.

**Q: Can customers pay partially and later?**  
A: Not by default, but can be configured. Contact support.

**Q: Do I need a separate merchant account?**  
A: Yes for payment gateways like JazzCash. We'll guide you through setup.

**Q: Is customer data secure?**  
A: Yes! PCI compliant and encrypted. We never store credit card details.
