- 50% of small businesses don't have a website at all

- Most business owners tried DIY builders and gave up
- Expensive agencies are out of reach for small budgets
- Freelancers are inconsistent or unavailable

**The Root Causes:**

**It's Too Hard**
- DIY builders are complex and unintuitive
- Design decisions are overwhelming
- Content writing takes weeks
- Technical setup is confusing

**It's Too Expensive**
- Agencies charge PKR 50,000-200,000+ upfront
- Hidden add-on costs appear later
- Ongoing maintenance is expensive
- Freelancers have inconsistent pricing

**It's Too Time-Consuming**
- DIY approach takes 60+ hours
- Business owners can't afford that time
- Agency timelines drag on for months
- Delayed launches mean delayed revenue

**Result:** Most small businesses either:
- Never launch a website (lost customers)
- Launch a DIY website that looks amateur (hurts credibility)
- Pay for a website they don't understand (no control)
