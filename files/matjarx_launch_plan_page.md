## Frequently Asked Questions

### Launch Plan Questions

#### How many pages are included?

**5-10 professional pages.** This typically includes:
- Home page
- About page
- Services/Products page
- Contact page
- Blog page (optional)
- FAQ page (optional)
- 1-3 additional pages based on your needs

**Need more pages?** Upgrade to Boost (20-30 pages) or Growth (50 pages).

#### Can I add pages later?

**Yes.** Add pages anytime through the website editor, or request help from our team. First 30 days include unlimited edits; after that, major changes may have additional fees.

#### What about the domain cost after the first year?

**First year included.** Year 2+ requires annual renewal (typically PKR 1,500-3,500 depending on domain). Paid through your MatjarX account.

#### Can I upgrade to a higher plan?

**Yes, anytime.** If you upgrade to Boost or Growth:
- New setup fee applies for new plan
- Old plan cancels
- Previous work transfers

#### Is hosting really included?

**100% included.** No separate hosting fees. Everything is included:
- Server costs
- Domain costs
- Email hosting
- SSL certificate
- Backups and security

#### What if I need more email accounts?

**First 5 are included.** Additional email accounts (6+) are PKR 500/month each. Contact support to add.

#### Do you offer phone support?

**Not on Launch Plan.** Email and WhatsApp support included. Upgrade to Boost or Growth for VIP phone support.

#### Can I get blog posts with Launch?

**No blog posts included.** Launch is basic web presence. If you need blog posts, upgrade to Boost (1 blog/month) or Growth (3 blogs/month).

#### What if I cancel?

**Cancel anytime.** Provide 30 days written notice via email. Refund policy applies; setup fees are non-refundable once work starts.

#### Can I get a money-back guarantee?

**Yes.** If you're unsatisfied within 24 hours and we haven't substantially started work, full refund available.

#### How is my data secure?

**Industry-standard security:**
- SSL/HTTPS encryption
- Daily backups
- Malware scanning
- Security patches
- DDoS protection
- Access controls

#### What happens if there's an emergency?

**Emergency support available.** Critical issues (downtime, hacking, data loss) get priority. Response within a few hours for critical issues.

#### Can I migrate to a different host later?

**Yes, you own your website.** After full payment, you can:
- Export your website
- Download your content
- Migrate to another host
- Export your email
- Take complete ownership

#### What about SEO?

**Basic SEO included:**
- SEO-friendly structure
- Mobile optimization
- Fast loading
- Google Analytics setup
- Meta tags and sitemaps

**Advanced SEO?** Upgrade to Boost or Growth for SEO services.

#### Is there a setup deadline?

**No deadline.** Launch whenever ready. Pay setup fee only when ready to start.

#### Can I buy my own domain?

**Bring your own domain:** If you own a domain, we can use it. No domain fee charged.
