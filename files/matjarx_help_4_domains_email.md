## Connecting Your Custom Domain

### Step-by-Step Connection Guide

**You have:**
- Your MatjarX website
- A custom domain (owned or registered elsewhere)

**Goal:** Connect them

### If We Registered Your Domain

**It's already connected!** No action needed.

Your website works immediately:
- yourcompany.com live and working
- Email working
- All set

### If You're Connecting Existing Domain

**Step 1: Unlock Your Domain**
- Log into your current domain provider
- Find "Unlock Domain" option
- Unlock it (prevents unauthorized transfer)

**Step 2: Get Transfer Code**
- In current provider account
- Find "Auth Code" or "EPP Code"
- Copy the code

**Step 3: Initiate Transfer**
- In MatjarX account
- Go to "Domains"
- Click "Transfer Domain"
- Enter domain name
- Paste transfer code
- Confirm

**Step 4: Authorization**
- Email sent to domain registrant
- Click confirmation link in email
- Approve transfer

**Step 5: Activation**
- Takes 24-48 hours
- You'll receive email when complete
- Domain now points to MatjarX
- Website accessible via your domain

### If You Just Want to Point Domain

**If you want to keep domain elsewhere, just point it to us:**

1. In MatjarX: "Domains" → "Point Domain"
2. We provide nameserver details
3. In your domain provider account: Update nameservers
4. Takes 24-48 hours to activate
5. Website accessible via your domain

## Email Accounts

### Setting Up Professional Email

### What's Included

**Launch Plan:** 5 email accounts  
**Boost Plan:** 10 email accounts  
**Growth Plan:** 15 email accounts  
**Platinum Plan:** 25 email accounts  
**Custom:** As needed

### Creating Email Accounts

**To create an email account:**

1. Go to "Domains" in your account
2. Click "Email Accounts"
3. Click "Add Email"
4. Enter email name (e.g., "sales")
5. Full email: sales@yourcompany.com
6. Set password
7. Save

**Email is instantly active**

### Accessing Your Email

**Via Webmail (browser):**

1. Go to mail.matjarx.com
2. Enter your email address
3. Enter password
4. Webmail opens
5. Send/receive emails

**Or use email client:**
- Outlook
- Gmail
- Apple Mail
- Thunderbird

### Email Client Setup

**To add email to Outlook/Gmail/etc:**

**IMAP Settings:**
- Server: mail.matjarx.com
- Port: 993
- Security: SSL/TLS
- Username: your email address
- Password: your password

**SMTP Settings:**
- Server: mail.matjarx.com
- Port: 465
- Security: SSL/TLS
- Username: your email address
- Password: your password

## DNS & Advanced Settings

### What is DNS?

DNS (Domain Name System) points your domain to your website.

**For most users:** Everything is automatic

**For advanced users:** We provide DNS details for custom configurations

### DNS Records

**Standard DNS records:**
- A Record: Points domain to server
- MX Record: Points email to email server
- CNAME Record: Aliases and subdomains

**If you need custom DNS:**
1. Go to "Domains" → "DNS"
2. View current DNS records
3. Contact support for changes

### Subdomains

**To create subdomain (e.g., blog.yourcompany.com):**

1. Go to "Domains"
2. Click "Add Subdomain"
3. Enter subdomain name
4. Save
5. Subdomain created

## Troubleshooting

### Domain Not Working

**If your domain doesn't connect:**

1. Check domain is registered
2. Wait 48 hours (DNS propagation)
3. Try different browser/device
4. Clear browser cache
5. Contact support if still not working

### Email Not Working

**If you can't access email:**

1. Check email address is created
2. Verify password is correct
3. Try webmail (mail.matjarx.com)
4. Check with email client settings
5. Contact support

### Slow Email or Delivery Issues

1. Check internet connection
2. Try different email client
3. Clear browser cache
4. Contact support if issue persists

## FAQ

**Q: Can I have multiple domains?**  
A: Yes! Connect multiple domains to different sites or same site.

A: Depends on your plan (5-25 accounts included).

A: Yes! Additional accounts at ~PKR 500/month each.

**Q: Can I rename an email account?**  
A: No, but you can create new one and delete old one.

**Q: Can I delete an email account?**  
A: Yes! Go to Email Accounts → Delete. All emails in that account are deleted.
