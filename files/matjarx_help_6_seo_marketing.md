## Understanding SEO

### What is SEO?

SEO (Search Engine Optimization) helps your website rank on Google so customers can find you.

**Benefits:**
- Free traffic from Google
- Targeted visitors
- Customers actively looking for you
- Long-term results
- Builds credibility

### Why SEO Matters

**Most people:** Start by searching on Google  
**Your goal:** Appear in top results for relevant keywords  
**Result:** Customers find you and contact you

## On-Page SEO

### Optimizing Your Pages

**Title Tags (60 characters):**
- Include main keyword
- Keep it under 60 characters
- Make it compelling
- Example: "Best Pizza Restaurant in Karachi | Luigi's"

**Meta Descriptions (155 characters):**
- Summarize page content
- Include keyword naturally
- Keep under 155 characters
- Make it click-worthy
- Example: "Authentic Italian pizza in Karachi. Fresh ingredients, wood-fired oven. Order online or visit us today."

**Headings (H1, H2, H3):**
- Use one H1 per page (main title)
- Use H2s for sections
- Include keywords naturally
- Help structure content

**Content (300+ words):**
- Write substantial content (at least 300 words)
- Include keywords naturally
- Use clear language
- Answer customer questions
- Update regularly

### Keyword Optimization

**Finding keywords:**
- What would customers search for?
- "Best [service] in [city]"
- "[Service] near me"
- "[Service] prices"
- Use Google Keyword Planner (free)

**Using keywords:**
- Include in page title
- Include in meta description
- Use in headings
- Use in first paragraph
- Don't over-use (sounds unnatural)

### Internal Linking

**Link to related pages:**
- Helps Google understand site structure
- Helps visitors navigate
- Keeps people on your site
- Spreads authority

**Example:** On a blog about "Web Design Tips," link to your Web Design Services page

## Local SEO

### What is Local SEO?

Local SEO helps you rank for location-based searches (e.g., "Dentist near me")

**Benefits:**
- Rank in local search results
- Show up on Google Maps
- Attract nearby customers
- Build local presence

### Google Business Profile

**What it is:**
- Free Google listing for your business
- Shows on Google Maps
- Appears in local search
- Displays reviews, photos, hours

**Setup:**
1. Go to google.com/business
2. Create or claim your profile
3. Add business information
4. Verify your address (postcard from Google)
5. Add photos
6. Gather reviews

### Local SEO Optimization

**Business information:**
- Accurate name
- Complete address
- Phone number
- Email
- Website link
- Business hours
- Accurate category

**Photos:**
- Business exterior
- Interior/workspace
- Products/services
- Team photos
- Customer photos
- At least 10 photos

**Reviews:**
- Ask customers to review
- Respond to all reviews
- Thank 5-star reviewers
- Address 1-star reviews professionally
- Build review count (more = better)

**Citations:**
- List business in directories
- Google Maps
- Local business directories
- Industry-specific directories
- Facebook Business

### Ranking Locally

**Factors affecting local ranking:**
- Reviews and ratings
- Google Business Profile completeness
- Citations and consistency
- Local keyword optimization
- Proximity to searcher
- Content relevance

## Backlink Building

### What are Backlinks?

Backlinks are links from other websites to yours.

**Why they matter:**
- Signal authority to Google
- Boost rankings
- Build credibility
- Drive referral traffic

### Building Quality Backlinks

**Do:**
- ✅ Write quality content worth linking to
- ✅ Request links from industry partners
- ✅ Submit to local business directories
- ✅ Get reviews (generates backlinks)
- ✅ Write guest posts
- ✅ Build relationships with other sites

**Don't:**
- ❌ Buy backlinks
- ❌ Exchange links indiscriminately
- ❌ Use link schemes
- ❌ Accept links from spammy sites

### Link Building Strategy

**Opportunities:**
1. Local directories (list your business)
2. Industry associations (member listings)
3. Sponsorships (get website link)
4. Guest blogging (write for other sites)
5. Media mentions (get coverage)
6. Partnerships (collaborate with others)

## Analytics & Tracking

### Google Analytics

**What to track:**
- Traffic sources (organic, direct, referral)
- Top pages
- User behavior
- Bounce rate
- Time on page
- Conversions

### Key Metrics

**Organic Traffic:** Visitors from Google search  
**Bounce Rate:** % of visitors who leave without action  
**Average Time on Page:** How long people stay  
**Conversions:** Actions you care about (contact, purchase, etc.)

### Using Data

**Optimize based on:**
- Top performing pages (create similar content)
- Pages with high bounce rate (improve or remove)
- Keywords that rank (expand content)
- Conversion data (improve conversion pages)
