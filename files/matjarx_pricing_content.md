## Frequently Asked Questions

### Everything You Need to Know About Our Plans & Pricing

#### Can I switch or upgrade my plan after I start?

**Absolutely! You can upgrade or downgrade anytime.** When switching plans, you'll receive either a prorated charge (if upgrading) or credits (if downgrading), calculated fairly based on what you've already paid.

Many customers start with Boost and upgrade to Growth after 2-3 months once they see results. Some scale to Platinum when they launch e-commerce. There's no penalty—just a simple switch.

#### Do I need to sign a long-term contract?

**No long-term contract required.** All plans are month-to-month or yearly/two-year (with discounts). You can cancel anytime without penalty.

The yearly and two-year options give you significant savings (2-6 months free), but there's no obligation to commit. Choose what fits your budget and comfort level.

#### Is there a money-back guarantee if I'm not satisfied?

**Yes—30-day full refund guarantee.** If you're unhappy with your website or service for any reason during your first 30 days, just email our customer success team for a refund. No questions asked. No gotchas.

We're confident you'll love what we build, but we want you to feel risk-free trying us.

#### How do I get started with website pricing and plans?

**Simple:** 
1. Choose the plan that fits your business stage (Boost, Growth, Platinum, or Custom)
2. Select your billing cycle (Monthly, Yearly with 2 months free, or Two-Year with 6 months free)
3. Complete checkout
4. Fill out your business questionnaire (15-30 minutes)
5. We build your website (7 days)
6. Launch call & training (30 minutes)

That's it. No hidden steps. No surprise charges.

Need help choosing? Call us at +92 303 372 0953 or email office@matjarx.com. Our team can walk you through which plan matches your business best.

#### What if my business needs are between two plans?

**That's what the Custom plan is for.** We can build you a plan with:
- More pages than standard
- More content (blogs, social posts)
- Extra features or integrations
- Specific growth strategies tailored to your business

Just [contact us] and we'll scope out a plan that's perfect for you.

#### Are there any hidden fees or extra charges?

**No hidden fees.** Your quoted price includes:
- Website design and content
- Setup and launch
- Domain (included or connect your own)
- Email addresses
- Support
- All features listed for your plan

Payment gateways (JazzCash, EasyPaisa, SadaPay, PayFast, credit cards) charge their own transaction fees, but MatjarX takes 0% commission on your sales. No hidden MatjarX transaction fees.

#### What about technical skills? Do I need to know code?

**No technical skills required.** Our easy-to-use editor lets you update text, images, and content without coding. For complex changes, your plan includes professional edit support.

Growth and Platinum plans also include ongoing website management, so we handle the technical side while you focus on your business.

#### Can I cancel my subscription if I change my mind?

**Yes, cancellation is simple.** Manage your subscription directly from your Business Hub—no phone calls or emails required (though we're always here to help).

Remember: **30-day money-back guarantee**. If you're unhappy in your first month, just request a refund and you're done.
