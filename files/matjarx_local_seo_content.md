#### Do you guarantee results?

If you don't see significant improvement in 3-6 months, we'll adjust strategy or discuss other options.

#### What's the difference between Local SEO and regular SEO?

**Local SEO** = Optimizing to rank in a specific geographic area (your city or service area). Focus on Google Business Profile, Maps, local keywords.

**Regular SEO** = Optimizing to rank nationally or globally for broader keywords.

**We do both:** Your MatjarX website is optimized for both local and broader keywords, giving you visibility in your area AND nationally.

#### Can you help if I already have reviews?

**Absolutely.** We'll:
- Migrate your existing reviews to your new Google Business Profile
- Continue collecting more reviews
- Respond to all reviews professionally
- Showcase them on your website

More reviews = higher ranking and better conversions. We help you build on your existing reputation.

#### What if my business doesn't have a physical location?

**If you serve customers at their locations** (plumber, electrician, hairdresser who goes to clients):
- We set up your service area on Google Business Profile
- Customers in your service zone see you in Maps and search results
- You get "near me" visibility without a physical storefront

**If you're fully online** (coaching, consulting, digital services):
- We optimize your website for keywords
- Local SEO is less relevant, but we focus on national/global SEO
- Different strategy, same goal: get found online
