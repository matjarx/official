## Frequently Asked Questions

### Common Questions Answered

**Q: How do I make changes to my website?**  
A: Use our website editor. Log in, click "Edit Website," and make changes. They save automatically.  
[Detailed guide →](/help/website-editor/)

**Q: How do I set up my custom domain?**  
A: In account settings, go to "Domains" and follow the connection guide. Takes 24-48 hours.  
[Full instructions →](/help/domains-and-email/)

**Q: Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan?**  
A: Yes, anytime from your account settings. Changes take effect on next billing date.  
[How to switch plans →](/help/billing-and-plans/)

**Q: How do I accept payments online?**  
A: Enable payment gateway (JazzCash or bank transfer). Setup takes 15-30 minutes.  
[Payment setup guide →](/help/ecommerce-and-payments/)

**Q: Can I cancel my plan?**  
A: Yes, anytime with 30 days notice. Your site stays live during notice period.  
[Cancellation guide →](/help/billing-and-plans/)

**Q: How do I improve my Google rankings?**  
A: Publish regular blog content, optimize your pages, and build local presence.  
[SEO tips →](/help/seo-and-marketing/)

**Q: I forgot my password. What do I do?**  
A: Click "Forgot Password" on login page and follow email instructions.  
[Password reset →](/help/account-and-security/)

**Q: How do I create email accounts?**  
A: In domain settings, create email accounts with your custom domain.  
[Email setup →](/help/domains-and-email/)
