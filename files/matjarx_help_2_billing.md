## Billing Cycle

### When You're Charged

**First Invoice:**
- Setup fee (one-time)
- First month's service

**Monthly Invoices:**
- Charged on the same day each month
- Same billing date as signup
- Invoice emailed before charge

### Payment Methods

**Accepted:** Bank transfer only

**How to pay:**
1. Receive invoice via email
2. Transfer amount to provided account
3. Include invoice number in transfer
4. Confirmation sent within 24 hours

### Grace Period

- 5-day grace period for overdue invoices
- After 5 days: Services may be suspended
- Pay to reactivate immediately

## Managing Your Plan

### How to Change Your Plan

**From your dashboard:**
1. Go to "Account Settings"
2. Click "Billing & Plans"
3. Choose "Upgrade" or "Downgrade"
4. Select new plan
5. Confirm changes

**New pricing applies** on your next billing date (or immediately for upgrades)

### Upgrading Your Plan

**When you upgrade:**
- New features activate immediately
- Higher pricing takes effect next billing cycle
- OR Pro-rata charge if you want features now

**Plans you can upgrade to:**
- Launch → Boost, Growth, Platinum
- Boost → Growth, Platinum
- Growth → Platinum
- Any plan → Custom

### Downgrading Your Plan

**When you downgrade:**
- Downgrade takes effect next billing cycle
- Current plan continues until renewal
- You won't lose any pages or content (if new plan accommodates)

**Things to know:**
- Some features may not be available in lower plan
- Excess pages can be archived
- Blog posts can be downloaded

**Example:** Downgrading from Growth (50 pages) to Boost (30 pages):
- Choose which 30 pages to keep
- Archive other 20 pages
- Adjust blog frequency (3/month → 1/month)

## Cancellation

### How to Cancel Your Plan

**To cancel:**
1. Email office@matjarx.com
2. Subject: "Plan Cancellation"
3. Provide 30 days written notice
4. State your cancellation date

**What happens:**
- Services remain active for 30 days
- Your website stays live
- Access continues until cancellation date
- No refund for remaining period

### After Cancellation

**Your website:**
- Remains live for 30 days
- After 30 days: Taken offline
- Can be migrated to another provider
- Files can be exported

### Data After Cancellation

After cancellation:
- 30 days: Active and accessible
- 90 days: Backup stored
- 1 year: May be permanently deleted

**Export your data** before cancellation deadline if you plan to use elsewhere

## Refund Policy

### Setup Fees

**Setup fees are non-refundable once work starts**

However:
- Within 24 hours of payment
- If no work has begun
- Full refund available

**After 24 hours or once work starts:** Non-refundable

### Monthly Subscriptions

**Monthly subscriptions are non-refundable** once the billing cycle begins

You can cancel anytime with 30 days notice (services remain active during notice period)

### Custom Projects

**Refunds considered for:**
- Significant dissatisfaction
- Work doesn't meet specs
- Approval hasn't been given

**Deductions applied for:**
- Time and labor invested
- Assets purchased
- Tools and resources
- Operational costs

Remaining balance refunded within 30 days

### Non-Refundable Items

❌ Domain purchases  
❌ Hosting fees  
❌ Third-party plugins/tools  
❌ Completed and approved work  
❌ Blog posts already delivered  
❌ SEO work performed

## Payment Issues

### What if my payment fails?

**If your bank transfer doesn't go through:**

1. Check the account details in your invoice
2. Verify amount and reference number
3. Try the transfer again
4. Contact your bank for issues

**If payment is unsuccessful after 5 days:**
- Services may be suspended
- You'll receive reminder emails
- Make payment to reactivate

### Disputing a Charge

**If you believe you were charged incorrectly:**

1. Email office@matjarx.com
2. Provide invoice number and details
3. Explain the issue
4. We'll review within 48 hours

### Payment Plans

**Flexible payment options available:**
- Full upfront (discount available)
- 50/50 (half upfront, half on launch)
- Milestone-based payments
- Monthly payment plan (discuss with sales)

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: When am I charged?**  
A: Setup fee at signup. Then monthly on your billing date (same day each month).

**Q: Can I switch plans mid-month?**  
A: Yes. New pricing takes effect next billing cycle (or immediately if upgrading).

**Q: What if I want to cancel?**  
A: Anytime with 30 days written notice. Your site stays live during notice period.

**Q: Can I get a refund?**  
A: Setup fees aren't refundable once work starts. Monthly subscriptions aren't refundable. See full refund policy for details.

**Q: Do you accept credit cards?**  
A: No. Bank transfer only. We'll send you transfer details in your invoice.

**Q: Can I pay differently?**  
A: Discuss payment plans with our sales team. Flexible options available.

**Q: What if I'm dissatisfied?**  
A: We focus on your satisfaction. Discuss concerns with your account manager.
