## 4. Approved Work Policy

### Final and Non-Refundable

**Any work that has been approved—verbally or in writing—is final and non-refundable under all circumstances.**

**Approval includes:**
- Verbal approval (phone call, WhatsApp conversation)
- Written approval (email, message confirmation)
- Signed approval on project documentation
- Published content on your website
- Implemented features or functionality

**Once approved, you cannot request a refund for that specific work.**

### Example Scenarios

**Scenario 1:** You approve website design via email. Later, you want different colors.  
**Result:** Not refundable (already approved)

**Scenario 2:** You approve a blog post in a WhatsApp conversation. Later, you want it rewritten.  
**Result:** Not refundable (already approved). Revision would be a new service.

**Scenario 3:** A feature is implemented and you test it. You ask for changes and approve the revised version.  
**Result:** Not refundable (approved in its final form)

## 8. Project Suspension and Non-Payment

### Payment Reminders

If a client fails to make payment, MatjarX will attempt contact through:
- WhatsApp messages
- Email notifications
- Phone calls
- Direct meetings

### Grace Period

A grace period is provided before service suspension (typically 5 days, though specific periods may be defined in your service agreement).

### Service Suspension

**After grace period expires, services may be suspended:**
- Website access may be restricted
- Email services may be suspended
- Support services may be halted
- Platform access may be limited

### Website Offline Status

**After 3 months of non-payment:**
- Your website may be taken offline
- Website files may be stored but not publicly accessible
- Hosting and domain services may be suspended

### Data Deletion Policy

**After 1 year of inactivity or non-payment:**
- Website data may be permanently deleted
- Database backups may be removed
- Website files may be permanently removed
- Data recovery may not be possible

### Reactivation After Suspension

To restore suspended projects, clients may be required to:
- **Pay all outstanding invoices** (principal amount)
- **Pay reactivation fees** (if applicable)
- **Pay updated service charges** (for storage or restoration)

**Warning:** After 1 year of suspension, a project may require complete redevelopment rather than simple reactivation. Redevelopment costs may apply.
