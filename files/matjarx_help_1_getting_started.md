## Welcome to MatjarX

### Your Website Launch Begins Here

Getting started is simple. We'll guide you through every step from signup to launch.

**Timeline:** 7-45 days depending on your plan  
**Total time commitment:** 2-3 hours over the onboarding period

---

## Step 1: Sign Up (10 minutes)

### Create Your MatjarX Account

**Visit matjarx.com and click "Get Started"**

**Enter:**
- Email address
- Password
- Business name
- Business type

**Choose your plan** (Launch, Boost, Growth, Platinum, or Custom)

**Complete payment** for setup fee + first month

**Check your email** for welcome message and next steps

---

## Step 2: Complete Website Questionnaire (45 minutes)

### Tell Us About Your Business

You'll receive an email with a questionnaire link. This helps us build your website correctly.

**Sections to complete:**

**Business Information**
- Business name and tagline
- What you do (description)
- Years in business
- Target customer
- Geographic service area

**Website Goals**
- Main goal (leads, sales, information)
- What visitors should do on your site
- Your success metrics

**Content & Design**
- Brand colors or preferences
- Logo (if you have one)
- Photos to include
- Websites you like
- Competitor sites (for reference)

**Technical Needs**
- Custom domain? (We can register one)
- Email accounts needed?
- Special features?
- Payment processing?

### Tips for Better Results

✅ Be specific and detailed  
✅ Gather photos, logos, and content  
✅ Have competitor URLs ready  
✅ Answer all questions  
✅ Don't rush—takes 30-45 minutes

---

## Step 3: Schedule Launch Call (5 minutes)

### Meet Your MatjarX Team

**A 30-45 minute video call to discuss your website**

**What to expect:**
- Introduction to your account manager
- Questions about your vision
- Clarifying timeline and next steps
- Discussing design and features
- Answering your questions

**How to schedule:**
1. Check your welcome email
2. Click calendar link
3. Choose time (Mon-Sat, 11 AM - 8 PM PKT)
4. Confirm

**Have ready:**
- Your completed questionnaire
- Photos or logo
- List of key pages
- Any special requirements

---

## Step 4: Build Phase (7-45 days)

### What Happens During Build

**Week 1:**
- Website structure designed
- Pages laid out
- Content entered
- Design theme applied

**Week 2-3 (depending on plan):**
- Pages refined
- Features configured
- Email accounts created (if applicable)
- Integrations setup

**Week 4+ (Platinum & Custom):**
- Advanced features
- Complex integrations
- Optimization
- Testing

### What You Do

**Provide Content & Materials:**
- Write business description
- Send photos and logos
- Confirm service/product info
- Provide team bios

**Review & Approve:**
- Check design mockups
- Review content
- Provide feedback
- Approve before launch

### Communication

**You'll receive:**
- Email updates every 3-5 days
- Preview links for review
- Questions via WhatsApp or email
- Response within 24-48 hours

---

## Step 5: Pre-Launch Review (2-3 days)

### Final Check Before Going Live

**You'll receive:** A preview link to review everything

**Check:**
- ✅ All pages display correctly
- ✅ Mobile design looks good
- ✅ Forms work properly
- ✅ Email functions (if applicable)
- ✅ Payment works (if applicable)
- ✅ All images load
- ✅ Links work

**Report any issues** and we'll fix them immediately

---

## Step 6: Launch! (1 day)

### Your Website Goes Live

**What happens:**
- Domain activates
- Website deployed to live servers
- Analytics activated
- Google Search Console setup
- Confirmation email sent

**Your website is now live and visible to the world!**

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How long until my website is live?**  
A: Typically 7-45 days depending on your plan. Launch Plan takes 7 days, Platinum takes 30-45 days.

**Q: What if I don't have content ready?**  
A: We can write it for you (additional cost) or we can launch with placeholder content you fill in later.

**Q: Can I edit my website after launch?**  
A: Yes! You can edit anytime using our editor, or request our help.

**Q: What if I'm not happy with the design?**  
A: We include feedback rounds during the build. You approve before launch, so you control the final result.

**Q: Do I need to know how to use the website?**  
A: No! We can make changes for you, or we can train you to edit it yourself.
